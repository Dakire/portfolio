_Written on 6 November 2026. Pricing and versions: official pages read on 6 and 7 November 2026, to be checked before you decide._

## My starting point

I started with AI through ChatGPT in November 2022, as soon as the conversational version came out.

## What it does well

- **Fast answers on a large number of subjects**, with features around text: files, images, voice, memory, scheduled tasks depending on the plan (official plans page).
- **A script skeleton** or a command from a clear description.
- **Rewrite, summarize, translate** a procedure or a message.

## Where I stay wary

- It answers with confidence **even when it makes things up**: a command option that does not exist, an imaginary module parameter. Check the vendor's documentation.
- **The offer changes often**: names, tiers and limits move. A screenshot of a price goes stale within weeks.
- The official page says the **Go plan "may include ads"**.

## IT use cases

| Need              | What I ask it                                                         | What I check                                         |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| PowerShell script | "List mailboxes last accessed more than 90 days ago, as CSV"          | Cmdlets and parameters on Microsoft Learn, `-WhatIf` |
| Troubleshooting   | "Here is an error (anonymized); which causes, in what order to test?" | Each lead on the machine, not on trust               |
| Documentation     | "Turn these notes into a numbered procedure"                          | That nothing was added or dropped                    |
| Reading logs      | "Summarize this excerpt (addresses replaced)"                         | The original excerpt                                 |

## Privacy and GDPR

- **Do not paste** secrets, clients' personal data, message contents or internal diagrams into a consumer plan.
- **Anonymize** before pasting (`client-A`, `192.0.2.10`, `example.com`).
- **For professional use**, look at the Business or Enterprise plan: the official page lists per-user plans starting at 2 users. Read the terms on data use before deciding.
- Check in your account settings what applies to history and to model improvement.

## How I use it

I use ChatGPT daily, alongside Claude and Gemini: text correction, quick fact checks, development.

## What to do

1. Try the free plan on questions with no sensitive data before paying.
2. Anonymize everything you paste, every time.
3. Test any script on a test account or machine (`-WhatIf`).
4. For a team, choose a professional plan and write a one-page usage rule.
