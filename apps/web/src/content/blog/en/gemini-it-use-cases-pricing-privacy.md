_Written on 6 November 2026. Pricing and versions: official pages read that day, to be checked before you decide._

## My starting point

I use it at work and personally, through Google Workspace, because the limits are higher there.

## What it does well

- **Fits into Google Workspace**: the plans page mentions Gmail, Docs, Drive and NotebookLM depending on the plan. For an organization already on Workspace, that is its strength.
- **A large context**: the page states a one-million-token window for the Pro plan, useful for long documents.
- **A free way in** with a Google account.

## Where I stay wary

- **Names and tiers change** (Plus, Pro, Ultra at two levels): what "Pro" contains is not what it contained six months ago.
- Limits are expressed **as multiples** of the free plan (2 times, 4 times) and reset every five hours up to a weekly cap: hard to know in advance what you will get.
- Like every assistant, it can make up a command: check.

What I do with it: tidy up meeting notes in Google Docs, and get help with Google Sheets formulas.

## IT use cases

| Need             | What I ask it                           | What I check                                           |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Google Workspace | A GAM command or an administration rule | The command in the GAM wiki, on a test                 |
| Documentation    | Summarize a long procedure document     | The figures and names it repeats                       |
| Email            | Explain an anonymized message header    | The header in the [analyzer](/en/tools/email-headers/) |

## Privacy and GDPR

- **Do not paste** personal data, secrets or message contents into the consumer plan.
- In a company Google Workspace, **the admin console** decides who can use what: check the settings and the terms that apply to your domain before opening access to the team.
- Anonymize before pasting.

## Pricing and versions (Google's French page read on 6 November 2026)

| Plan                  | Price per month | What the page says                                                 |
| --------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Free                  | €0              | Flash-Lite model, 15 GB of storage                                 |
| Google AI Plus        | €4.99           | Limits doubled versus free, 400 GB of storage                      |
| Google AI Pro         | €21.99          | Limits multiplied by 4, 5 TB of storage, one-million-token context |
| Google AI Ultra (5x)  | €99.99          | Limits 5 times those of Pro                                        |
| Google AI Ultra (20x) | €219.99         | Limits 20 times those of Pro                                       |

The page names the "Gemini 4 Argon" model in the paid plans. These names will change: check the page before you decide.

## How I use it

I use Gemini with Google Workspace Business Standard (€13.60 excl. VAT per month). It is on by default there; with Business Starter licences, it is less present across the Google suite.

## What to do

1. If you are on Workspace, try it first where your data already is.
2. Compare plans on the French page, in euros.
3. Anonymize what you paste.
4. Have the person who administers the domain approve opening it to the team.
