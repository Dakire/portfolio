_Written on 6 November 2026. Pricing and versions: official pages read that day, to be checked before you decide._

## My starting point

I tested Copilot on a compatible PC.

## What it does well

- **Work inside the apps**: Microsoft's page mentions Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams.
- **Draw on the organization's data**: results rely on what the user is allowed to see.
- **A free way in**: Copilot Chat is offered at no extra cost with eligible Microsoft 365 subscriptions, by signing in with a work account.

## Where I stay wary

- **It exposes bad permissions.** If a SharePoint folder is open to everyone by mistake, Copilot will find it for everyone. Clean up sharing **before** deployment.
- **The cost adds up** on top of the Microsoft 365 licence, and some advanced features are billed by usage on top.
- Like the others, it can be wrong: a meeting or document summary needs proofreading.

I have not seen Copilot surface a badly shared document, and I have not deployed it at any client: my feedback stops at a personal trial.

## IT use cases

| Need          | What I ask it                                           | What I check                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Email         | Summarize a long thread in Outlook                      | The decisions and the dates            |
| Documentation | Format a procedure in Word                              | That nothing was made up               |
| Spreadsheets  | An Excel formula from a description                     | The result on a sample                 |
| Security      | No blind trust: it does not replace a permissions audit | Sharing, with the administration tools |

## Privacy and GDPR

- Microsoft's page presents Copilot Business with **enterprise-grade data protection and governance**: read the terms and the tenant documentation before deployment.
- **Audit permissions** (SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, shared mailboxes) before turning the licence on: see [Securing Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace](/en/blog/secure-microsoft-365-google-workspace-baseline-settings/).
- Tell the people concerned what is processed and by whom; consult your data protection contact about an impact assessment if the scope is wide.

## How I use it

I would not pay for the licence, neither for myself nor for a client: I find it too limited.

## What to do

1. **Start with permissions**, not licences: a half-day sharing audit avoids nasty surprises.
2. Test Copilot Chat with a small group, with no extra licence.
3. Only pay for the licence for people who really use it.
4. Document the usage rule for the team.
